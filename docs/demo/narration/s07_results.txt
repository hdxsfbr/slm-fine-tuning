The numbers. On thirty hand-written challenge conversations, judged by the same rubric as the ablation, the base passes none, the best frontier prompt passes twenty percent, the first two tuned models fifty and seventy, and the submitted model, trained on twelve hundred and ninety-eight conversations from a frontier teacher, seventy-seven: every pressure conversation and six of the thirteen mixed ones.

On one hundred and fifty-five held-out conversations it passes ninety-two percent. A data-efficiency curve over four sizes and two seeds shows that half the data holds the pressure and only the full set holds the mixed conversations, and a second judge family, read on the earlier model, keeps the ordering: base, then prompt, then tuned.
